Dynamic Mental Health Monitoring for Victims of Atrocities

A continuous, multi-channel check-in system that turns a victim's ongoing wellbeing — through the investigation, trial, and rehabilitation process — into a trackable, explainable Distress Score, so counsellors and district officials can act before a crisis rather than after one.

How it works

1. Periodic check-ins

Chatbot, IVRS call, SMS, mobile app or web portal reaches the victim on a set schedule, in their language, with a short conversational flow.

2. Signal extraction

Text, mood, and engagement behaviour (missed or delayed replies) are analysed for emotion and distress signals.

3. Dynamic Distress Score

A weighted, explainable score (0–100) is generated per check-in and tracked as a longitudinal trend, not a one-off snapshot.

4. Risk prediction

Rate-of-change and pattern shifts are used to flag likely escalation before the score itself reaches crisis level.

5. Alerts & routing

Crossing a threshold notifies the counsellor and district nodal officer automatically, with a suggested intervention.

6. Dashboards

District, State and National views for monitoring vulnerable and high-risk cases, with per-case explainability.

Prototype scope: this build demonstrates the full check-in → scoring → alerting → dashboard loop with a rule-based scoring engine standing in for the production NLP / Emotion AI / Voice Stress models (MuRIL / IndicBERT / Whisper + openSMILE), so the architecture and explainability chain can be evaluated end to end. All data here is local-browser demo data — see README for the production data & privacy design (DPDP Act compliance, encryption, role-based access, consent).
